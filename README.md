# Free Chess Courses for Everyone

**All of my chess courses are free to read, study, and download.** I share them to help people understand chess and enjoy learning it. I do not monetize this project.

Welcome to the menu of my **36 chess courses, collections, archives, and study tools**. Read online, save a book for later, or choose a skill you want to practice. Everyone is welcome.

**[Beginner lessons](#beginner-lessons) · [Practical workbooks](#practical-workbooks) · [Guided practice](#guided-practice) · [Openings and strategy](#openings-and-strategy) · [Larger collections](#larger-collections) · [Offline libraries](#offline-libraries) · [More chess resources](#more-chess-resources)**

**Not sure where to start?** Learn the basics with [Chess, Clearly](https://1d42c4.github.io/chess15/), build a safety habit with [Stop Hanging Pieces](https://1d42c4.github.io/chess-safe-moves/), or explore plans with [Make a Plan](https://1d42c4.github.io/chess13/). For a White opening repertoire, start with the [1.d4 / 2.c4 study library](https://1d42c4.github.io/d4-c4-repertoire/).

**Using this menu:** **Read** opens a course website. **Files** opens its books, downloads, and reading guide on GitHub. Some collections overlap; choose the subject and format that suit you.

## Make your study time count

Choose one lesson, try its position before reading the answer, and explain the opponent's reply. Write down one idea to look for in your next game. Revisit difficult positions before moving on.

The library includes AI-created lessons and credited resources by other authors. Each collection provides its own sources and study notes. The books in Danya's archive are publisher samples; the column collection is listed separately. Please follow the included credits and reuse terms when sharing material.

**Found something that needs correcting?** Leave an issue in the relevant repository with the course name, file or page, and the position or move involved.
